Paper Trail
How Paper Trail works, and what it can't do yet

It splits the draft into sentences and pairs each one with the source sentences that share its most distinctive words, numbers and names.

It flags what a reader would care about: absolutes the source doesn't use ("all", "unanimously"), numbers, names and quotes that don't match, and careful words the draft drops ("eligible", "may", "up to", "about", "until", "said").

It never decides what's true. The editor decides. Paper Trail records the decision, the wording change, the source and who checked it.

What you paste stays in this browser tab. There's no account, no server and no AI service. The page loads only its fonts from Google Fonts.

Limits: matching is word-based, so a heavy paraphrase can pair with the wrong passage; pick the right one by hand. English only. Paste text or add a .txt file; PDF and Word import are planned.

Paper Trail is a working title. Prototype v0.2, November 2026, by Zeyad Genena. Free and open source.
