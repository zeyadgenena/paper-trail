Paper TrailCheck AI-assisted copy against its sources
How this prototype works, and what it can't do yet

It splits the draft into sentences and pairs each one with the source sentences that share its most distinctive words, numbers and names.

It flags differences a reader would care about: absolutes the source doesn't use ("all", "unanimously"), numbers, names and quotes that don't match, and careful words the draft drops ("eligible", "may", "up to", "about", "until", "said").

It never decides what's true. The editor decides, and Paper Trail records the decision, the wording change, the source and who checked it.

Everything stays in this browser tab. There is no account, no server and no AI service. Nothing you paste is uploaded.

Limits: matching is word-based, so a heavy paraphrase can pair with the wrong passage (pick the right one by hand). English only. Paste text; PDF import is planned.

Paper Trail is a working title. Prototype v0.1, November 2026, by Zeyad Genena.
